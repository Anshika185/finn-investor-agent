**Subject:** ENAM - IIT screening - 2027

Hello ENAM team,

I built FINN, a mobile-first investor intelligence prototype focused on the daily briefing and on-demand research workflows. The Today view brings high-priority changes forward, while the full feed lets the user search and filter by company, category, and priority. Alert settings expose price-move and unusual-volume thresholds. The Research Desk organizes a company view around business context, financial trajectory, cash quality, balance-sheet risk, management guidance versus reported outcomes, and a bull/bear debate.

My main product decision was to make prioritization the first screen and keep routine paperwork available but collapsed. I chose a responsive web prototype with no setup step so it is easy to try on a phone. The research view is designed around an evidence trail and primary company documents rather than secondary commentary.

I deliberately kept the data layer out of this prototype: the displayed prices, filings, financial figures, and source excerpts are illustrative demo content and are not verified live data. The next step would be to connect Yahoo Finance end-of-day history, NSE/BSE announcement feeds, and annual reports and earnings-call transcripts, preserving source links and timestamps for each claim; then deploy the service and replace all fixtures with verified records.
